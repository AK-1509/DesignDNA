Principles

  1. Make long reading comfortable on screen · 1 source
  2. Wide margins frame every spread · 1 source
  3. One accent color per spread · 1 source
  4. Let the canvas breathe: on average 55% of each page is left empty. · measured
  5. Color is structural, not decorative: ~44% of the corpus is chromatic. · measured
  6. Typography does the heavy lifting; imagery is occasional. · measured
  7. Hold body text to a ~68-character measure. · measured

Pull quotes borrow the display face and hang off an accent rule.

Emphasis comes from type and space before color, which matches how the corpus uses its palette.

Voice & rules

precisewrywarm

Do

  • Run photographs full bleed
  • Pair Playfair Display (display) with Source Serif 4 (text). Don't swap their roles.

Don’t

  • No drop shadows
  • Don't introduce new hues. The palette is orange-red + dark blue + tinted neutrals.
